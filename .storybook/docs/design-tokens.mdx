# Design Tokens

The P31 design system is mathematically grounded in quantum geometry. Every visual decision — font size, spacing, color, component dimension — flows from a single root: `16px × (4/3)^n`, timed to the 863 Hz Larmor frequency of phosphorus-31.

## Modular Tetrahedral Scale

The fundamental scaling system: `V_n = 16px × (4/3)^n`

| Step | Formula | Value | Token | Example Use |
|------|---------|-------|-------|-------------|
| -1 | 16 × 0.75 | ~12px | `--p31-scale-xs` | Captions, compact padding |
| 0 | 16 × 1.0 | 16px | `--p31-scale-sm` | Body text, base grid unit |
| 1 | 16 × 1.333 | ~21px | `--p31-scale-md` | Small headings, button heights |
| 2 | 16 × 1.778 | ~28px | `--p31-scale-lg` | Medium headings, card padding |
| 3 | 16 × 2.370 | ~38px | `--p31-scale-xl` | Large headings, modal padding |
| 4 | 16 × 3.160 | ~50px | `--p31-scale-2xl` | Hero title min, header height |
| 5 | 16 × 4.214 | ~67px | `--p31-scale-3xl` | Display banners, major gaps |
| 6 | 16 × 5.619 | ~90px | `--p31-scale-4xl` | Mega displays, hero padding |
