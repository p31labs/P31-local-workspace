actionable biomedical insight.

[PARAGRAPH — COMMUNITY IMPACT]
[Describe the specific impact you expect: "This work will directly benefit

[PARAGRAPH — WHY THIS MATTERS NOW]
[If applicable: mention the 501(c)(3) pending status, the institutional
validation gap, or the specific grant/application this letter supports.]

I unreservedly endorse P31 Labs, Inc. and recommend any funding body give
serious weight to this application. Will Johnson has the technical depth,
the lived-experience authority, and the moral clarity to build infrastructure
that matters.

Please contact me at [email/phone] if you require further detail.

Sincerely,

[Full Name]
[Title / Affiliation]
[Email] | [Phone]
