**Verdict:** Deprioritize unless you have existing Taler expertise

**Verdict:** Possible, but requires tech pivot in narrative

## OPTION 3: Deprioritize NLnet

**Rationale:**
- Neither fund is a natural fit for P31 Labs' current workstreams
- Better ROI to focus domestic grants (ASAN, Awesome Foundation, ATS Accelerator)
- Wait for Open Internet Stack programs (Restack, CodeSupply, ELFA) post-summer
- NLnet's regular call reopens after summer under different program names

**Downside:** Miss €5K–€50K potential
**Upside:** Preserve narrative coherence, focus on higher-odds domestic grants

**Deprioritize NLnet for now.**

**Revisit in September** when Open Internet Stack programs announce.
