Mission & About

Every neurodivergent family invents workarounds for a world not designed for them. P31 Labs turns those workarounds into shared, open-source tools.

Mission
To build free, open-source assistive technology that honors the sovereignty of neurodivergent families. Every tool is designed for the reality of family life — not the abstraction of it.
Vision
A world where neurodivergent families flourish with tools that adapt to them — not the other way around. Where care work is seen, valued, and rewarded.
Values
Sovereignty — You own your data. No tracking, no ads, no extraction.
Care-First — Every product grounded in care science, designed for real family dynamics.
Open Source — MIT licensed. Free forever. Auditable by anyone.
Spoon-Aware — Interfaces adapt to your capacity, from crisis to flow.
Principles
• Build for the most vulnerable first
• Design for crisis as a default state
• Measure success in spoons saved, not revenue
• Share everything — code, research, lessons
• Stay small, stay sovereign, stay accountable

LOVE Protocol

LOVE ($LOVE) is a non-extractive care-credit protocol. Each hour of verified care coordination, transport, advocacy, or sensory regulation generates LOVE rewards recorded on the P31 LOVE ledger. Rewards are redeemable within the care mesh for respite, tools, or community support.
